## FINDING A - The exact steps match validates both pipelines

3,279 vs 3,279.0. JADE's daily aggregates are computed from the same sensor stream my pipeline reads, and my ingest→transform→marts chain reproduces its number precisely. That is a genuine mutual validation and it belongs in the case study: **where JADE has the data, JADE is accurate.**

## FINDING B - "JADE missed it" is the wrong diagnosis. The ring was not worn.

**Correction to my own first pass (2026-08-07).** I initially wrote that the assistant *invented* the football session. **That was wrong, and I retract it.** The user's own prompt opened the session: *"Activate jade protocol, played football for 30-40 mins from 11:20-12am roughly."* The football was **self-reported by the user**. I inferred fabrication from the assistant's output alone, without the prompt in view - and asserted it in a findings file. Logged in full below, because it is the third instance of the pattern this study is about.

What the assistant actually wrote: *"Your football session is recorded as a manual correction, since **JADE clearly missed it** - the app shows 0 active minutes and Workout Minutes N/A for the day."*

Ground truth for July 6, 21:00 → 00:10 local: **zero motion, zero steps, and not a single heart-rate reading.** Bins carry ~6 readings each, none of them HR, motion flat at 0 - the signature of a ring off-body or on the charger, not of a worn ring detecting stillness.

**So JADE did not "miss" anything. The ring recorded nothing, and JADE reported 0 active minutes - correctly.** The assistant's error is the diagnosis: it attributed a **sensor-coverage gap** to an **AI failure**. That is precisely the distinction this whole case study turns on - *"my ring never recorded this"* versus *"the AI can't see what my ring recorded"* - collapsed in the wrong direction, and then written into a committed log.

Unresolved: the export holds football on **July 7, 18:57-19:47** (`ACTIVITY Football (Soccer)`, two `NATIVE_WORKOUT` rows, 77.9 workout-minutes). Either that is a separate session from the unrecorded 11:20 PM game, or the reported time was off. **Only my own recollection can settle it** - and the fact that neither JADE, nor the assistant, nor the raw record can settle it from data alone is itself the finding.

**Recommendation: do not merge that PR as written.** The July 6 entry should say the session went unrecorded because the ring was not worn - not that JADE missed it.

### The pattern, three times over

| # | Who | Premise asserted without checking | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | JADE | "you're on a shift work schedule" | Real citations, wrong person |
| 2 | The logging assistant | "JADE clearly missed it" | Wear-gap misfiled as product failure, committed to a repo |
| 3 | **Me, in this file's first draft** | "the assistant invented the football" | A false accusation, written into the findings |

Same shape every time: a plausible story, never tested against the raw record, then propagated with the authority of whatever artifact carried it. **This belongs in the published write-up.** An audit that catches the failure only in the audited system, and not in its own instruments, has not understood the failure.

## FINDING C - On sleep, JADE is right and my pipeline is wrong

JADE: 4.9 h from a 2:40 AM bedtime. Mine: 11.83 h starting 20:00 on July 5 - but that row carries `start_truncated=1`, the flag added precisely because a block beginning at the 20:00 window edge has an unreliable bedtime and an inflated duration. July 5 has only 20 bins of data (a near-total non-wear day), so the "sleep" block spans dead air.

Wake times agree (07:50 vs ~7:30). **The truncation flag did its job - and this is the first external confirmation that nights carrying it must be excluded from any duration or bedtime claim.** Report it: an audit that only finds fault in the audited system is not an audit.

## FINDING D - ⚠️ HRV units are not comparable (corrects a live probe)

JADE reports HRV as **61 ms**. My `raw_hrv_2` field has p50 133, p95 237, and a July 6 mean of 135.5. Values of 133-237 ms RMSSD are not physiologically plausible for a healthy young adult; 61 ms is. **`raw_hrv_2` is therefore not RMSSD-in-milliseconds - it is a different metric or scale.**

**Consequence for P10, which is still unrun:** the challenge prompt quotes "3,046 non-zero HRV readings, **mean 135.3**." The **count is sound** and the false-negative finding stands - the readings exist and JADE said they didn't. The **mean must be dropped**: quoting 135.3 as milliseconds against JADE's 61 ms baseline invites a correct rebuttal and forfeits the probe. P10 has been edited accordingly.

More broadly: **no absolute HRV value from this export may be published as milliseconds** until the field's units are established. The count-based and directional findings are unaffected.
